# Code of Conduct

Lapilli follows the [CNCF Community Code of Conduct](https://github.com/cncf/foundation/blob/main/code-of-conduct.md),
which itself is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).

## Our pledge

In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming environment, we as contributors and
maintainers pledge to make participation in our project and our community a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

## Scope and enforcement

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces and in public spaces when an
individual is representing the project or its community.

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior can be reported to
**`conduct@cncf.io`**, the CNCF Code of Conduct Committee. Use that first if the report
concerns a maintainer, or if you would rather it not reach this project — Lapilli has one
maintainer today, so "report to the maintainers" and "report to the person involved" are
usually the same address, and nobody should have to weigh that.

Reports may also go to the maintainers listed in [`MAINTAINERS.md`](MAINTAINERS.md), who are
obligated to respect the privacy and security of a reporter. What they cannot offer is a
guaranteed response time: with a single maintainer this is best effort, and `conduct@cncf.io`
is the escalation for anything that needs more than that.

For the full text, expected behavior, and enforcement guidelines, see the
[CNCF Community Code of Conduct](https://github.com/cncf/foundation/blob/main/code-of-conduct.md).
